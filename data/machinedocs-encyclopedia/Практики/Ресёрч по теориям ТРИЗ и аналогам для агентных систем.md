# Ресёрч по теориям ТРИЗ и аналогам для агентных систем

> [!abstract] Scope and evidence statement
> Это отдельный обзор инструментов/теорий классической ТРИЗ и их ближайших software/process analogues.
> Документ отвечает: что именно утверждает метод, где у него релевантный аналог в software engineering,
> что можно передать агентам, какие источники подтверждают процедуру и какие — outcome claims. Обзор не
> является доказательством, что ТРИЗ/аналоги улучшают multi-agent coding в целом. По найденным источникам
> прямых независимых сравнительных испытаний всего набора методов на agentic software development нет.

## 1. Executive synthesis

- **Сильнее всего domain-specific для software architecture:** QAW → ADD → ATAM. SEI reports ясно
  специфицируют их различные роли; это method guidance, не доказательство universal causal effect.
- **ТРИЗ — семейство теории + средств**, а не только matrix/40 principles. MATRIZ ARIZ-85C содержит
  problem modeling, resource analysis, IFR/physical contradiction, knowledge search, reformulation and
  solution analysis. Matrix is a small, accessible route; ARIZ is an iterative analysis program.
- **Наиболее переносимые TRIZ constructs к ПО:** structure problem without solution bias; articulate opposing
  states; inspect system boundaries/functions/resources; generate mechanism-diverse hypotheses; judge them
  against measurable intended outcome and side effects. This is an engineering synthesis, not universal law.
- **Наименее straightforward transfer:** physical/mechanical parameter matrix and literal substance/field
  metaphors. Digital systems add semantics, access control, distributed state, stochastic behavior and versioned
  dependencies. Software-specific characteristics need empirical validation.
- **Empirical evidence for creativity is nonzero but narrow:** an ASME student ideation study found higher
  novelty/variety with TRIZ vs ad-hoc generation, with quantity slightly lower; narrow design task/student
  context means no inference to correctness, production quality or coding agents.
- **SIT evidence:** engineering training study involved 110 professionals but a combined 30-hour SIT +
  self-regulated-learning workshop; cannot isolate SIT or translate to agentic software outcomes.
- **Other analogues answer different questions:** QFD/QAW requirements, Double Diamond discovery, DMAIC/A3
  process improvement, TOC flow constraint, FMEA/FTA risk, GMA design-space structure, TDD/fitness functions
  validation. They are not all “TRIZ variants.”
- **For agents:** automate evidence gathering, enumeration, consistency checks and artifacts; protect the
  policy/approval/test oracle boundaries deterministically. Method selection and findings remain hypotheses
  until owner review + local eval/measurement.

## 2. Research method and limitations of this register

Sources were sought across: classical TRIZ primary materials / recognized-body-of-knowledge pages, peer-reviewed
or academic reports, method-owner or professional practice pages, software-specific applications, and applied
examples. Source status is labeled below rather than flattening them into a single authority ranking.

This is a targeted research register, not a systematic review with a reproducible database/query protocol,
formal inclusion/exclusion and independent dual coding. Search availability is language- and access-biased;
non-English TRIZ literature and books are underrepresented. Claims are limited to what each cited source supports.
Links to repositories and tools are implementation examples only and can change.

### Evidence labels

| Label | Supports | Does not automatically support |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary standard/method specification** | Definition, steps, intended use | That method works better than alternatives |
| **Peer-reviewed empirical study** | Narrow finding under its study design/context | Transfer to another domain/population/system |
| **Case study / application** | Feasibility and described artifacts in one context | Causal attribution, general outcome effect |
| **Review** | Map of published work and research gap (quality depends on search/synthesis) | Independent validation of every underlying study |
| **Method-owner / vendor description** | Owner’s own terminology and method | Neutral comparison or independent benefit evidence |
| **Practical synthesis** | Reasoned adaptation and implementation advice | Empirical result; needs local validation |

## 3. TRIZ architecture: separating philosophy, method and tools

### What is in scope

The Altshuller Institute’s TRIZ Body of Knowledge page (which describes a jointly developed body of knowledge
with MATRIZ/ETRIA representatives) names: IFR/ideality; administrative, engineering and physical
contradictions; system evolution trends; 40 principles and contradiction matrix; Su-Field/76 standards; ARIZ;
scientific effects. MATRIZ’s ARIZ-85C text describes an iterative algorithm with 9 parts (the label ARIZ-85C
is not a claim that the method has exactly 85 elementary actions).

Other useful classical/modern TRIZ tools commonly included in practice: function analysis, resource analysis,
trimming, cause-effect chain analysis, system operator/Nine Windows, flow analysis, effect/function-oriented
search, feature transfer and psychological operators (e.g., Small Smart People). Schools/versions differ in
terminology and grouping; do not treat one taxonomy as a universal standard.

### Why this matters for software

Calling all of these “theory” hides whether a task asks for: problem model; idea generation; architecture
assessment; statistical process improvement; risk analysis; or executable proof. Tool selection must follow
question shape. A tool cannot be credited with an outcome it does not evaluate: 40 principles suggest search
directions; they do not benchmark a patch.

## 4. Deep review: TRIZ concepts and tools

### 4.1 Administrative, technical and physical contradictions

**Method definition.** Administrative contradiction is an expressed need without a known means; technical/
engineering contradiction associates an improvement with a worsening characteristic; physical contradiction
requires opposite states of a system element/property. ARIZ formulates both states in plain language and may
refine a vague need through the analysis. The precise distinctions vary in TRIZ literature, so preserve the
source’s terminology in research notes.

**Software mapping.** Translate into explicit system outcomes and mechanisms: task success vs token/latency;
security restriction vs legitimate task completion; concurrency vs integration conflict; autonomy vs control.
Do not treat any two competing goals as a validated causal conflict. A measured mechanism, e.g. shared CPU
saturation, matters. Otherwise write “unverified tradeoff hypothesis.”

**Agent use.** Multiple agents can independently formulate contradictory views from the same verified evidence;
owner chooses scope and priority. Store both opposing states, metric, workload, mechanism hypothesis and
falsifier. Avoid model self-confirmation and “the problem must fit a contradiction” bias.

**Evidence status.** Contradictions are core TRIZ concepts in the body of knowledge and ARIZ specification.
Evidence for universal applicability to software systems is not established merely by that definition. For
software, translate each parameter set to repository/SLO-specific measures; no verified universal mapping table
was located for every software architecture property.

### 4.2 39×39 contradiction matrix and 40 inventive principles

**Method definition.** Matrix relates a selected improving and worsening parameter to historically compiled
principles. MATRIZ Level-1 manual outlines pairwise formulation, parameter identification, matrix lookup and
translation of a selected principle into concrete candidate solutions. The principles are abstract prompts,
not implementation recipes. Later TRIZ developments put more emphasis on physical contradictions, Su-Field
and other tools, despite matrix popularity.

**Software fit.** Digital systems involve semantic properties (authorization, data lineage, API stability,
consistency, maintainability, privacy) whose meaning is unlike many mechanical matrix parameters. Lippert &
Cloutier discuss this gap and propose digital characteristics/principles; their paper is a conceptual adaptation,
not a validated universal matrix or software intervention trial. Govindarajan et al. review software TRIZ and
characterize the software application literature as early-stage.

**Useful analogues.** SIT for concise ideation prompts; architecture tactics/patterns for solution mechanisms;
GMA for combinations; QAW/ADD to make quality drivers explicit. None is interchangeable with matrix lookup.

**Agent use.** Let an agent suggest a principle only with a natural-language mechanism, evidence path and
alternative non-TRIZ mechanism. Human deduplicates and checks applicability. Generate options even if no matrix
row fits. Score via outcomes/architecture/safety tests rather than principle count.

**Evidence status.** One peer-reviewed student study (Hernandez et al., ASME JMD 2013, DOI below) compared
TRIZ ideation with ad-hoc idea generation for an LED traffic-light snow issue and reports higher novelty and
variety, slightly lower quantity. The sample/task are narrow; this does not demonstrate production software
correctness, innovation value, effort savings or agent benefits. Other reported training studies are also
context-bound.

### 4.3 Physical contradictions and separation principles

**Method definition.** Separate contrary properties by time, space, condition or system structure/part-whole;
ARIZ also considers system levels/resources and other paths. The separation is a search heuristic to break an
all-or-nothing demand.

**Software analogue.** Enforce separation by policy boundary rather than metaphor: repository/tenant ACL,
read-vs-write identity, task context vs durable memory, draft vs approved config, sandbox vs production, risk-tier
approval, canary vs full rollout, context loaded on trigger vs always-on. A physical split might have logical
interaction complexity; test that separation itself does not cause stale data or divergent state.

**Analogue relation.** Architecture partitioning, least privilege, information-flow control, tiered autonomy,
CQRS/event separation and progressive delivery are engineering mechanisms, not one unified “separation theory”.
Use the relevant native practice for implementation and assurance.

**Evidence status.** The separation taxonomy is method guidance; specific digital boundary mechanisms have
software testing/security methods. No evidence source found that establishes “apply separation principles”
by itself reduces agent harm.

### 4.4 Ideal Final Result and ideality

**Method definition.** IFR is a target image of required useful effect with minimal added components/sacrifices;
ideality expresses a direction balancing useful functions against cost/harm. It is a problem framing heuristic,
not a mathematically objective scalar over all software quality.

**Software analogue.** Quality scenario + SLO/acceptance criteria + explicit externalities: task outcomes,
security, reliability, latency, engineering/reviewer time, compute, maintenance. High-level “self-managing
agents” must be rewritten as tests, authority constraints and rollback conditions.

**Evidence status.** Core concept in TRIZ sources. No single accepted universal software ideality formula or
causal proof that IFR statements increase code quality was identified. Do not optimize a summed score unless
weights and unacceptable-risk constraints are explicitly owned.

### 4.5 Function analysis and functional modeling

**Method definition.** Model components and their useful/insufficient/excessive/harmful actions to reveal
function gaps and conflict zones. TRIZ/software literature argues software can be included in models; Beckmann’s
software-modelling work explores functional modeling for software/IT systems.

**Software implementation.** Maintain four linked views, each with provenance: source-level static dependency;
runtime traces/events; data/control-flow/trust boundaries; user value journey. Include actor, action, object,
condition, effect, source commit and confidence. Graph-derived facts and intended business semantics are
separate layers.

**Analogues.** Architecture/dependency graph, call graph, distributed tracing, DFD/taint, value-stream map,
service blueprint. Graphs can show structure/sequence, not causal efficacy automatically.

**Agent use.** A graph builder may extract from AST/OpenAPI/trace; a critic detects omissions; module owners
verify intent. Retrieval can cite nodes/edges rather than hand agents whole-repository narratives. A changing
code graph must be pinned to commit/version.

### 4.6 Substance-Field analysis and 76 Standard Solutions

**Method definition.** Su-Field models problematic interaction of two substances through a field; classify the
interaction (e.g., harmful, insufficient, excessive) and select a class of transformations in the 76 Standards.
MATRIZ explains standards as a library of patterns with a shared problem/solution representation.

**Software adaptation.** Use typed interaction, not a vague physics metaphor: `(actor/component, target/data,
channel/action, observed effect, trust boundary, evidence)`. For a harmful interaction, generate alternatives such
as interceptor/policy gateway, deduplication, feedback, monitor, mediation, decoupling or a contract — then map
to actual design patterns, security architecture and tests.

**Analogues.** FMEA/control catalog, architecture tactics, security patterns, failure/feedback patterns, API
contracts. These catalogs can be specific, versioned and executable, which makes them more direct for software.

**Evidence status.** MATRIZ is primary for procedure; Navas 2013 and method texts describe applications; no
controlled evidence found demonstrating effectiveness of 76 standards in agentic codebase decisions. Do not
report a generated Su-Field as factual causality without trace/test evidence.

### 4.7 ARIZ

**Method definition.** MATRIZ-published ARIZ-85C text defines sequential steps to transform vague situation to
mini-problem/problem model, analyze conflict and resources, formulate IFR/physical contradiction, use knowledge
base, change/reformulate problem if needed and analyze solution/process. Multiple cycles may be required.

**Software analogue.** No exact equivalent. A pragmatic composite is:

- uncertain product need → Double Diamond;
- quality attributes/architecture design → QAW → ADD → ATAM;
- recurrent measurable process defect → A3/PDCA or DMAIC;
- high consequence risk → threat model + FMEA/FTA;
- candidate combination search → GMA/SIT;
- actual implementation proof → executable tests/benchmarks/rollout.

Calling that composite “software ARIZ” would overstate equivalence; call it an ARIZ-inspired staged workflow if
explicitly using the source logic of problem model, resources, contradiction, reformulation and verification.

**Evidence status.** Primary source gives algorithm specification. The complexity of expert use and historical
claims in training materials are not controlled evidence that ARIZ outperforms simpler tools. No multi-agent
ARIZ study found.

### 4.8 Resource analysis and trimming

**Method definition.** Inventory resources in system/subsystem/supersystem and surroundings, including space,
time, matter/energy, information/functions, waste and existing infrastructure; trimming removes/transfers
component functions while preserving useful result. Exact resource taxonomies vary.

**Software analogue.** Find existing test fixtures, platform capabilities, observability, cached data, API
contracts, owner knowledge, unused schedulers and code already executing relevant validation. For trimming,
identify dead code/duplicated service/unused instructions and ask whether capability can disappear or be
transferred without violating trust/ownership/operability.

**Analogues.** Dead-code/dependency analysis, cost-of-quality, value-stream mapping, TOC exploit step, YAGNI,
refactoring and API minimization. Transfer functions carefully: a shared service may become a new bottleneck or
blast-radius multiplier.

**Agent use.** Search existing architecture catalog and build/test graphs before suggesting new tools. Require
candidate cost/maintenance/permissions and proof that existing invariant remains. Resource search must respect
ACL/license/privacy policies.

**Evidence status.** TRIZ procedure/practice sources plus software engineering analogues; not evidence that
“use all available resources” guarantees lower cost. Hidden maintenance risks and opportunity costs matter.

### 4.9 Cause-effect chain analysis (CECA) / root conflict analysis

**Method definition.** Expand from an observed harmful effect through causes and interactions toward a root
conflict/leverage point. Often a TRIZ-specific cause-effect chain includes useful/harmful functions and system
reformulation; distinct implementations use different diagrams/rules.

**Software analogue.** Blameless incident timeline/postmortem, causal graph, fault tree, DMAIC Analyze, change
history, traces, reproducible test. In distributed failures, use multiple contributing causes and avoid treating
“root cause” as a single metaphysical point.

**Agent use.** Evidence collectors cite exact trace/log/commit/repro. Causal agents return branch hypotheses,
confidence and falsification experiments. Do not use model-generated Five Whys chain as proof; verify arrows.

**Evidence status.** Root-cause methods are established practice, but each tool has context/limitations. No
validated claim that TRIZ CECA outperforms incident-analysis alternatives in software or agents was found.

### 4.10 Nine Windows / System Operator

**Method definition.** Structure thinking over subsystem/system/supersystem and past/present/future. It helps
surface external actors, missing levels and temporal evolution. It is not a causal model or a forecast by itself.

**Software application.** Construct views for agent/tool/workflow, service/repository/platform and team/user/org;
compare previous/current/target state, including interfaces and resources. For each claimed trend, collect
observable indicators, counter-scenarios and date for re-review.

**Analogue.** Architecture context maps, system dynamics, scenario planning, service blueprint and roadmap.
GMA can enumerate uncertain configurations; causal-loop diagrams express hypothesized influences.

**Evidence status.** TRIZ/author case literature describes use; identified examples are illustrative. No direct
controlled evidence of forecast accuracy for digital platform evolution.

### 4.11 Laws/trends of technical system evolution

**Method definition.** TRIZ trends include increasing ideality, dynamization, transition to supersystems,
mono-bi-poly and other lines. Some trend taxonomies differ among schools. Authors use trends as heuristics to
identify opportunity, forecast or check concept development.

**Digital caution.** Software evolves through product choices, standards, markets, security constraints,
organizational topology and path dependence. Trends are neither invariant laws for every domain nor an excuse
to predict inevitable autonomy/decentralization. Translate a trend to a contingent hypothesis with signals,
assumptions and alternative futures.

**Analogues.** Technology roadmapping, scenario planning, architecture fitness functions, ADR review triggers,
SRE/SLO trend monitoring and DORA capability measurement.

**Evidence status.** TRIZ body-of-knowledge shows these are core components; predictive validity in software
systems is not established by inclusion in the framework. Review and update against observation.

### 4.12 Scientific effects / Function-Oriented Search / feature transfer

**Method definition.** Search for a known effect that performs a target function or transfer an implementation
from another domain. In physical engineering, scientific-effect libraries provide mechanisms.

**Software analogue.** Search version-pinned algorithm catalogs, protocols, language/runtime primitives, existing
repository code, open standards and architecture tactics. The model may identify a candidate; source, API,
license, threat surface and benchmark must be verified.

**Agent-specific boundary.** A model’s pretrained recollection is not an authorized/up-to-date source catalog.
Use scoped retrieval with provenance, approved dependency policy and a prototype test.

**Evidence status.** TRIZ tool definition and case use; no reason to expect transferred physical effect patterns
will translate directly into code without domain model.

### 4.13 Psychological operators: Small Smart People, STC and others

**Method definition.** Visualization/prompts intend to overcome psychological inertia and reframe a problem.
They can be thinking aids, not objective models. Schools may include Small Smart People, size-time-cost
operator, Goldfish, and related techniques.

**Agent relevance.** LLMs may already produce multiple verbal framings; these prompts can act as diversity
controls (describe as micro-agents/objects, vary system boundary or time/scale) only if they yield a testable
new mechanism. More prompt tokens or persona variety does not establish cognitive diversity.

**Analogue.** Prompt-based perspective taking, adversarial review, design critique, counterfactual scenarios.
Separate genuinely independent evidence paths from role-play of a single model.

**Evidence status.** Heuristic-level. Direct tests in software agents were not located.

## 5. Research review: identified analogues

### 5.1 QAW, ADD, ATAM (architecture)

- **Source:** Barbacci, _SEI Architecture Analysis Techniques and When to Use Them_, CMU/SEI-2002-TN-005,
  https://doi.org/10.1184/R1/6583727.v1
- **What source says:** QAW analyzes conceptual/system architecture against critical quality attributes before
  the architecture is fully developed; ATAM can then reveal how design satisfies selected requirements and
  identify risks, sensitivities and tradeoffs.
- **Best fit:** architecture quality conflicts where stakeholder scenarios, candidate design decisions and
  risk analysis can be made explicit.
- **Evidence status:** authoritative method description; not an experiment comparing method outcomes.
- **Agent mapping:** agents draft scenario candidates/traceability, extract design evidence, construct risk
  questions; humans validate priorities and acceptable residual risk; prototypes/fitness functions verify.

### 5.2 QFD / House of Quality (requirements)

- **Source:** ISO 16355-1:2021 scope gives general principles/perspectives of QFD; ASQ House of Quality page:
  https://asq.org/quality-resources/house-of-quality
- **Software sources:** empirical application/report literature includes Haag, _A Field Study of the Use of QFD
  as Applied to Software Development_ (1992), and reports of Software-QFD/HoQ requirements engineering;
  a peer-reviewed or publicly accessible source must be selected per specific claim. QFD projects vary widely
  and relationships/weights often involve expert judgment.
- **What it does:** translate customer/stakeholder needs into product/technical characteristics and priorities.
- **Limits:** weighted matrix can create false precision and reflect selected interviewees/weights; does not
  prove user need or resolve engineering mechanisms. For software architecture quality scenario elicitation,
  QAW can be a more direct technique.
- **Agent mapping:** summarize raw feedback with quote/source, propose measurable technical characteristics,
  expose missing stakeholder segment. Never let an LLM invent voice-of-customer or determine priorities alone.

### 5.3 Double Diamond (problem discovery)

- **Source:** Design Council, https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/
- **What source says:** Discover/Define/Develop/Deliver as visual description; the diagram is not an instruction
  manual. Emphasis on understanding affected people, developing different options and testing at small scale.
- **Limits:** not RCA, architecture assessment or proof. Official source supports model description, not controlled
  causal impact on product success.
- **Agent mapping:** interview synthesis/theme candidates/prototype task generation; human researchers validate
  meanings/quotes and access; usability/behavior test validates.

### 5.4 A3/PDCA and DMAIC (continuous improvement)

- **Sources:** Lean Enterprise Institute A3 report, https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/a3-report/ ; ASQ DMAIC,
  https://asq.org/quality-resources/dmaic ; Lean Enterprise Institute PDCA/technical debt article,
  https://www.lean.org/the-lean-post/articles/how-to-wipe-out-its-technical-debt-with-pdca/
- **What sources support:** practical phase/process definitions; A3 as a learning/problem-solving/dialogue
  artifact; DMAIC for define/measure/analyze/improve/control of an existing measurable issue.
- **Limits:** method page/practice article is not statistical proof of software productivity. Data quality,
  operational definitions, confounding, sample design and control plan determine inference quality.
- **Agent mapping:** draft timeline, inspect measurements, cluster defect signatures; stats/causal claims need
  qualified review; outcome becomes regression/CI measure.

### 5.5 Theory of Constraints (system bottleneck)

- **Source used:** practitioner overview by Lean Six Sigma Groep, https://leansixsigmagroep.nl/en/lean-agile-and-six-sigma/theory-of-constraints-uk/ ; classic primary references include Goldratt’s _The Goal_ and later TOC works.
- **Claim:** five focusing steps identify/exploit/subordinate/elevate/repeat; measurement of queues and flow is
  required to identify a software delivery constraint.
- **Limits:** the cited practitioner overview is not a primary empirical study. Specific software/DevOps causal
  benefit claims require independent primary research with a clear baseline and comparison.
- **Relevant current contextual evidence:** Google Cloud DORA 2025 official announcement reports an
  observational relationship of AI adoption with increased delivery throughput but still a negative relationship
  with stability; it argues downstream feedback/testing/version-control weaknesses can be exposed. This is not a
  TOC trial and not evidence that a specific bottleneck is always code review. Source:
  https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-the-2025-dora-report
- **Agent mapping:** instrument queues, WIP, review, integration/CI capacity, rework; increase upstream generation
  only if the end-to-end metric improves or safety intent demands it.

### 5.6 FMEA / FMECA / fault tree / threat modeling (risk)

- **Sources:** NASA GSFC-HDBK-8004 (official handbook),
  https://standards.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/standards/GSFC/Baseline/0/GSFC-HDBK-8004_Approved_0.pdf ;
  IEC 60812 scope for FMEA; peer-reviewed critique/evaluation: _Failure mode and effects analysis outputs: are
  they valid?_, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3405478/
- **What sources support:** structured process/failure-mode/effect/control inventory. In the cited health-care
  FMEA evaluation, teams missed incident-database failures; multiplying ordinal S/O/D ratings was judged
  invalid and multidisciplinary/source-data input recommended.
- **Limits:** FMEA is knowledge-bounded, can miss interactions/emergent behavior; RPN can hide distinct risks.
  Use action priority/severity rules and rationale rather than numeric score as truth. Fault trees model event
  combinations; threat models address adversarial causes/trust boundaries; they complement FMEA.
- **Agent mapping:** agents enumerate failure modes from code/contracts/traces; enforce safety policy separately;
  specialist/owner review and tests required. No agent is allowed to waive a safety gate based on FMEA score.

### 5.7 GMA / morphological analysis (configuration space)

- **Primary/method source:** Ritchey, _Problem Structuring using computer-aided morphological analysis_, JORS
  57 (2006), 792–801, https://www.swemorph.com/pdf/psm-gma.pdf
- **What source says:** GMA structures multidimensional, usually non-quantified problem spaces via parameters
  and values; Cross-Consistency Assessment (CCA) evaluates pairwise compatibility. It is not based on causal
  relationships; judgments of compossibility reduce but do not validate the design field.
- **Software application:** Moore, _Using the Morphological Analysis Perspective (MAP) in Software..._
  (East Carolina University thesis repository),
  https://thescholarship.ecu.edu/bitstream/10342/5023/1/Moore_ecu_0600O_11522.pdf
  describes a software-engineering strategic problem application, morphological fields and pairwise CCA.
  It is a thesis/proof-of-concept, not broad comparative evidence.
- **Limits:** dimension design, omitted options and expert judgement shape result; combinations are not
  probabilities or causal effect estimates. CCA can give traceable reasoning and prune inconsistent pairs.
- **Agent mapping:** enumerate options, apply hard-coded compatibility rules, expose uncertain expert
  judgments; compile shortlisted configurations and test.

### 5.8 SIT / ASIT (TRIZ-derived ideation)

- **Method owner source:** https://www.sitsite.com/method/ ; terminology and five tools are described by
  SIT itself. SIT shares TRIZ lineage; do not treat it as independent theoretical validation of TRIZ.
- **Empirical study:** Barak & Albert, _Fostering Systematic Inventive Thinking (SIT) and Self-Regulated
  Learning (SRL) in Problem-Solving and Troubleshooting Processes among Engineering Experts in Industry_,
  Australasian Journal of Technology Education,
  https://ajte.org/index.php/AJTE/article/download/45/26/146
- **Design:** five groups, total n=110, 30-hour combined SIT + SRL workplace workshop; observed/reporting
  significant improvement in problem-identification and solution indicators.
- **Limits:** workshop combined with SRL; study context is engineering training, not randomized independent
  comparison of SIT tools in software agents. Outcomes include tests/questionnaires/observations; effect
  attribution/generalization cautious.
- **Agent mapping:** short transformations prompts can produce solution diversity; test usefulness/feasibility
  against a non-SIT baseline and report mechanism coverage. Not root-cause analysis.

### 5.9 TRIZ ideation effectiveness studies

**Hernandez, Schmidt & Okudan (2013), _Systematic Ideation Effectiveness Study of TRIZ_, Journal of
Mechanical Design 135(10), 101009. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4024976**

- Peer-reviewed study with two institutions (UTEP/UMD), student groups and same LED traffic-light snow
  design task.
- Reports TRIZ vs ad-hoc method improved measured novelty/variety; quantity slightly reduced (not significantly
  in extracted report). Study outcomes are idea quality metrics, not implementation quality, product value or
  time-to-production.
- Transfer limits: student population, design ideation task, specific training/process, metric validity and
  independent judging. It supports narrow ideation hypothesis, not agentic software productivity.

A second line (e.g. engineering creativity training/workplace field studies) suggests training can change idea
outcomes, but curricula/interventions and samples vary. Do not combine effect sizes without a systematic
meta-analysis.

### 5.10 Software-specific TRIZ research

**Govindarajan, Sheu & Mann (2019), _Review of Systematic Software Innovation Using TRIZ_, IJOSI 5(3),
72–90, https://doi.org/10.6977/IJoSI.201903_5(3).0006**

- Review of software design/development/coding/testing/maintenance applications.
- Authors state software TRIZ applications remain at an early stage relative to established engineering areas.
- Review is valuable domain map; scope/method quality and papers included should be considered before treating
  it as a systematic evidence synthesis. Its conclusion warns against strong transfer claims.

**Lippert & Cloutier (2019), _TRIZ for Digital Systems Engineering: New Characteristics and Principles
Redefined_, Systems 7(3), 39, https://doi.org/10.3390/systems7030039**

- Discusses mismatch of classical physical/mechanical parameters to digital systems; presents adapted
  characteristics/principles and an example.
- Authors explicitly state generic TRIZ solution triggers leave a gap to specific solution and cannot replace
  special domain knowledge.
- This is conceptual adaptation/system engineering example, not validation that its mapping is complete or
  improves software project outcomes.

**Kluender (2011), _TRIZ for software architecture_, Procedia Engineering 9, 708–713, DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.proeng.2011.03.159**

- Software architecture application/flight-simulator re-architecture, with partial mapping to software qualities.
- Case report supports feasibility of using TRIZ lens, not universal matrix validity or causal benefit.

**Beckmann, _Modeling Software in TRIZ_ / _Modelling of Software and IT systems in TRIZ_**

- Springer chapter (2022), https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-662-66201-4_1 and follow-up
  work considers functional modeling where software is component/object of analysis.
- Useful as a conceptual bridge for function models; not comparative agent study.

## 6. Comparison matrix: intended questions, directness and agent usage

| Tool/theory | Main question | Software-native fit | Agent-autonomy suitable for | Human accountability / verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40 principles + matrix | What alternative mechanism might resolve this technical conflict? | Low–medium unless remapped to validated domain characteristics | Candidate generation | Confirm mapping; prototype/benchmark |
| Physical contradiction/separation | Can opposing states be separated by time/place/condition/level? | Medium–high when translated to ACL/partition/workflow | Generate boundary designs | Security/policy owner; negative tests |
| IFR/ideality | What desired state minimizes sacrifice/new harmful functions? | Medium as goal framing | Draft outcome and guardrails | Product owner sets priorities/weights |
| Function analysis | Which actor/component creates useful/harmful effect? | High with code+runtime views | Evidence extraction/graphing | Validate missing dynamics/intent |
| Su-Field/76 standards | How to transform inadequate/harmful interaction? | Low–medium; mechanism translations needed | Generate candidates from model | Domain expert maps standards to software pattern |
| ARIZ | How to iteratively reframe and solve a hard inventive problem? | Medium as workflow scaffold, not direct codable algorithm | Facilitate stages, track unresolved issues | Owner validates each model and decision |
| Resources/trimming | What can be reused, removed or transferred? | High with static/runtime inventory | Search/code simplification proposals | Check hidden function/security boundary |
| CECA / root conflict | What cause chain produces undesirable outcome? | Medium, if evidence graph is real | Draft competing causal paths | Validate arrows via traces/tests/data |
| Nine Windows | What system levels/time horizons are missing? | Medium for architecture/product strategy | Produce structured context map | Validate stakeholders and future assumptions |
| Evolution trends | What plausible future direction should be considered? | Low as forecast; heuristic only | Generate scenarios/indicators | Human roadmap judgment; re-eval |
| Effects / function-oriented search | What known mechanism can perform target function? | High via repo/docs/API index | Retrieve source-backed analogues | API/license/security/benchmark verification |
| SIT | How to generate ideas under existing constraints? | Medium for quick ideation | Structured prompt transformations | Evaluate feasibility/value, recognize lineage |
| QAW/ADD/ATAM | Which quality scenarios drive design and what risks/tradeoffs result? | Very high | Draft scenarios/evidence/risks | Stakeholder priorities and owner decision |
| QFD | What customer needs map to technical characteristics? | High for requirements translation | Cluster feedback with provenance | Real-user sampling and weighting |
| DMAIC/A3/PDCA | Which process gap/cause/fix is supported by data? | High for recurring measured issues | Data prep, charts, action tracking | Statistical/causal interpretation and control owner |
| TOC | Which current constraint limits system outcome? | High if flow data exists | Queue/WIP analysis | Confirm global metric, test moving constraint |
| FMEA/FTA/threat model | What failures/attacks can harm assets and which controls? | High for high consequence domains | Enumerate candidates and link to tests | Security/safety review; gates enforce controls |
| GMA | Which combinations of qualitative design options are coherent? | High for complex architecture choice | Enumerate/computer-check explicit rules | Expert CCA, prototypes/eval |
| TDD/property/fitness | Is the change satisfying executable invariant? | Very high as verification layer | Write/run candidate tests carefully | Independent oracle review; full CI |

## 7. Practical synthesis for a multi-agent software factory

### Router decision sequence

1. **Classify unknown:** need, architecture quality, causal defect, process flow, safety risk, option space, or
   proof/verification.
2. **Check data readiness:** source commit, traces, reproduction, user sample, metrics, ownership; if absent,
   collect facts instead of inventing a theory.
3. **Select minimum sufficient method:** Double Diamond/QFD for need; QAW/ADD/ATAM for architecture;
   A3/DMAIC for recurrence; TOC for flow; threat/FMEA for risk; GMA/SIT/TRIZ for solution search;
   tests/benchmark for verification.
4. **Use TRIZ as optional overlay** only where a real conflict or stuck solution space exists.
5. **Raise method depth by uncertainty × impact:** no ARIZ workshop for trivial bug, no brainstorming replacing
   review of high-impact auth/data change.
6. **Lock acceptance and policy boundaries before implementation.** Agent making patch cannot unilaterally
   edit protected test or success criteria.

### Evidence contract per agent claim

```yaml
claim: "context retrieval misses cross-repo auth contract"
kind: hypothesis # observation | interpretation | hypothesis | proposal
source: "trace://run/92 + eval://case/ctx-15 + repo://sha/path"
commit: "full sha"
confidence: medium
counter_evidence: ["search logs show result was returned but summary omitted it"]
falsifier: "rerun held-out task with retrieval result pinned into context"
owner: platform-engineering
```

### Local evaluation protocol

- Test on representative past tasks/PRs/incidents; keep held-out tasks and hard negatives.
- Compare baseline with one methodological addition (e.g., free ideation vs contradiction prompt;
  plain ADR vs QAW/ADD; unstructured RCA vs evidence-linked A3).
- Freeze repo/model/tools/workload when possible; randomize run order; repeat nondeterministic trials.
- Blind independent evaluators for idea usefulness/novelty, diagnosis quality, and architecture risk when feasible.
- Score task correctness, scenario coverage, root-cause confirmation, risk escape, reviewer effort, cycle time,
  model/tool/CI cost and maintenance burden.
- Separate safety gates from randomized learning: do not trial unsafe permissions or removed controls on users.
- Publish uncertainty, failures, selection limits and effect segments; update benchmark only with reviewed cases.

## 8. Findings and limits in plain language

**Supported:** TRIZ is a structured body of problem framing and solution-search tools; a narrow controlled
ideation study supports novelty/variety benefit on a specific engineering task; methods such as SEI architecture
analysis give structured tools for architecture quality tradeoffs; other methods target user research, process
variation, risk and design-space consistency.

**Not supported by the evidence collected:** that TRIZ is universally superior to engineering judgment; that
classic mechanical contradiction matrix transfers completely to software; that any analogy method improves
agent success automatically; that multi-agent role splitting increases correctness; that increasing throughput
necessarily increases shipped value; that generated diagrams, FMEA scores or agent consensus certify a solution.

**Practical decision:** use TRIZ where its specific lens adds search value; prefer software-native/quantitative
methods for diagnosis, architecture assurance, security and verification. Treat all candidate changes as
hypotheses, and measure the complete human-agent delivery system locally.
